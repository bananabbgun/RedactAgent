# Real-layout check (public government forms, synthetic people)

All other corpora in this repository use layouts drawn by our own generators. This corpus uses **real published
forms** that were never seen during development, filled with **randomly generated** people, numbers, addresses and
dates. It checks whether RedactAgent generalises beyond our own layouts. It is not a test on real patient documents:
the content is still synthetic.

| Template | Form | Publisher | Pages | Language |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `nhi_referral_zh` | 全民健康保險院(所)轉診單 (NHI referral form, 附表二修正) | Ministry of Health and Welfare, Taiwan ([source](https://mohw.gov.tw/dl-9392-0c42e35a-94e3-42e5-a892-5056bf700c3c.html)) | 6 | zh |
| `nhi_card_zh` | 請領健保卡申請表 (NHI card application, 113.11) | National Health Insurance Administration, Taiwan ([source](https://www.nhi.gov.tw/ch/dl-67284-06928126dbb24356977da6fa1de1dae3-1.pdf)) | 4 | zh |
| `va_release_p1_en`, `va_release_p2_en` | VA Form 10-5345, Request for and Authorization to Release Health Information | US Department of Veterans Affairs ([source](https://www.va.gov/forms/10-5345/)) | 4 + 4 | en |
| `cms1500_en` | CMS-1500 Health Insurance Claim Form (the public copy carries a SAMPLE watermark, kept as published) | US Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services ([source](https://www.cms.gov/Medicare/Billing/ElectronicBillingEDITrans/1500)) | 4 | en |

22 pages, 287 personal-data items. Downloaded 2026-11-27; originals in `raw/`, 150 dpi renderings in `templates/`.

- Taiwanese pages: typed fill-ins (as printed by a hospital system), personal seals, an ID photo, a quasi-identifier phrasing
  that appears in no prompt and no other corpus. US pages: handwritten-style fill-ins (VA form), typed fill-ins
  (CMS-1500), ink signatures.
- Organisation data (hospital addresses, switchboard numbers, facility codes, tax IDs, provider NPIs) is not
  labelled as personal data, as in the other corpora.
- Generated by `scripts/make_real_layouts.py` (seed 1127). Same light scan simulation as the other corpora.

**Protocol, fixed before any result:** evaluated once with the final configuration (accuracy mode, strict policy,
the same command as v3); the result is reported as it comes out and is not used to change prompts, rules or settings.

```bash
python eval/evaluate.py --workers 8 --data data/real_layouts/corpus --out eval/results_b70/real_layouts
```

## Result (2026-11-28, the one run)

Run on a fresh clone deployed with `deploy/b70/up.sh` on the organiser's 2x Arc Pro B70 instance
(`eval/results_b70/real_layouts/`, summary by `eval/real_layouts_summary.py`).

| Form | Pages | Recall | Precision |
|---|---|---|---|
| NHI referral form | 6 | 79.6 % | 54.1 % |
| NHI card application | 4 | 88.2 % | 77.9 % |
| VA Form 10-5345, page 1 | 4 | 78.9 % | 88.6 % |
| VA Form 10-5345, page 2 | 4 | 96.4 % | 75.8 % |
| CMS-1500 (SAMPLE watermark) | 4 | 35.0 % | 52.6 % |
| **All** | **22** | **73.9 %** | **66.3 %** |

Rules only: 32.1 %. Misses: characters written one per printed box (ID numbers, postcodes), dates split into
year / month / day blanks and ROC slash dates, and most fields on CMS-1500. False alarms: hospital switchboard
numbers and e-mails, red form lines read as seals. Review triage (risk >= 70 or unread ink) flagged 20 of 22 pages,
holding 71 of the 75 missed items. Nothing was changed after seeing this result.

**Answer-key correction (2026-11-28, after the run).** Dates written into printed year / month / day blanks were
labelled as the digits only; the other corpora label the whole date including 民國 and 年/月/日 (e.g. 民國49年9月21日).
The key was corrected to that convention (`gt/`; the original is kept in `gt_original_digits_only/`), the images are
byte-identical, and the stored output of the one run was re-scored, not re-run
(`eval/results_b70/real_layouts_rescored/`): recall 73.9 % (unchanged), precision 66.3 % -> 70.9 %.
